HabitBalance is a private, no-account habit tracker for iPhone. Every habit, streak, and record stays on your device — no sign-up, no ads, no trackers. Track unlimited habits, completely free, forever.

Coming soon to the App Store

Everything a habit needs

Simple on day one, deep when you want it — and honest about what's free.

✅

Any kind of habit

Simple yes/no habits (Read, Meditate, No Sugar) and measurable ones with your own units and daily targets (Cups of Water, Pages Read, Minutes of Exercise).

📅

Flexible scheduling

Every day, specific weekdays, or "X times per Y days" — with per-habit reminder times that fit your routine, not a one-size-fits-all alarm.

🔥

Streaks & history

A running streak for every habit, an activity feed of your milestones, plus a color-coded calendar and heatmap of your recent weeks — at a glance.

📱

Widgets

Home Screen widgets show today's habits and streaks; the medium size has a tap-to-done list. A Lock Screen widget keeps your streak in sight.

🎙️

Siri Shortcuts

Mark a habit done, log a value, or open a habit's stats completely hands-free with Siri and the Shortcuts app.

💾

Backups that travel

Export your habits, records, and reminders to a file any time — and import password-protected backups from the Android version, so switching platforms never means starting over.

🎨

Made to fit you

Light, dark, or system theme with unlockable accent packs. Localized in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, and Russian.

♿

Accessible by design

Built with VoiceOver and Dynamic Type support from day one, not bolted on afterward.

✨

Optional AI assistant

Want a nudge? An optional subscription adds habit ideas and plain-English monthly summaries, powered by Anthropic's Claude — with a consent screen that shows exactly what's sent before anything ever leaves your device.

Private by architecture

Not a settings toggle — there is simply no account system, no analytics SDK, and no ad network in the app at all.
