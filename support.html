HabitBalance for iOS

Need help or found a bug? The fastest way to reach us is to open an issue on GitHub — no account data is needed because the app has none.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move my data from the Android version?

In the Android app, export a backup file. Then in HabitBalance for iOS, go to Settings → Database and use the Android import option, entering the password you chose when exporting. Habits, records, and reminders come across in one step.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. There is no account and no cloud copy — see the privacy policy for the full picture. Use Settings → Database → Export Data to create a backup file you control.

Is a subscription required?

No. The core app — unlimited habits, streaks, calendar and heatmap views, widgets, Shortcuts, and backups — is free. Advanced stats, full history editing, and premium themes are a single one-time purchase. Only the optional AI Assistant is a subscription, and the app is fully functional without it.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap the + button, and search for HabitBalance. The medium widget includes a tap-to-done list; the small and Lock Screen widgets show your habits and streak at a glance.

Which Siri Shortcuts are available?

Three: Mark Habit Done, Log Habit Value, and Open Habit — usable with Siri or in the Shortcuts app.

What languages does the app support?

English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, and Russian, with more planned.
